Dip-leg pipe ID (mm) 40.9 mm
Sets the pocket's cross-section, and the submerged discharge — Chiu thesis: the dip-leg's bent section "extends to below the bottom-most impeller", so the pocket forms in bulk liquid near the vessel bottom, not at the vapor space. Default sized to feed rate at a design velocity of 0.255 m/s — back-derived from Chiu thesis Table 7.2, where both real reactor tiers (40.9 mm ID/18 kg/min and 52.5 mm ID/28 kg/min) land within 4% of this same velocity at their peak feed. Recomputes with feed rate and feed temperature; override here if a real pipe ID is known.
Stagnant zone length (× pipe ID) 15×
How far down the dip-leg the submerged dead zone extends, in pipe diameters. No source for this multiplier — pure assumption; pocket carrier mass = zone volume × liquid density (shown below), not a fraction of the whole batch.
Valve-to-nozzle line length (m) 2.0 m
EO already standing in the line between the ESD valve and the nozzle keeps draining into the pocket after the valve closes, on top of feed×response-time — only applies to failure modes where the valve actually closes (A, C). This length is set by isolation-valve maintenance clearance (no bypass is installed for this CV, so it's a straight run, not a bypass loop), not by the dispersion physics that size the stagnant zone above; no source for a typical value here.
Pocket→bulk blend time (s) 60 s
Time constant for the pocket to homogenize into the bulk after the mixer restarts (turbulent blend time) — not the failure duration set above. No source; assumption. A fully physical (heat-transfer-coefficient × contact-area) version would need the polyether's liquid viscosity and thermal conductivity, which aren't sourced yet — see basis notes.
EO feed temperature (°C) 18 °C
Chiu thesis §7.3.1: EO header temperature is "almost always under 20 °C (15–20 °C)"; up to 30 °C possible for the first slug on a hot day.
Max fill at 100 % (vol %) 80 %
Chiu thesis Table 3.1: the 2-litre kinetics autoclave runs at 1600 mL of 2000 mL max working volume = 80 %. Used as the sourced default; no production-vessel fill % found in our sources.
