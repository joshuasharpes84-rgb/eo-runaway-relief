# EO Runaway Relief Calculator: Loss of Mixing (EG → PEG)

Models an ethylene oxide (EO) runaway in a KOH-catalyzed ethoxylation batch reactor (ethylene glycol
starter → polyethylene glycol) after **loss of agitation**. It returns the relieving rate over time, peak
relief load, vented mass, pressure and temperature histories, a Leung-omega / API 520 orifice size, and
alternatives (valve type, rupture disk, effluent and quench destination).

> **Make it yours, free:** download this repo (**Code → Download ZIP**, or `git clone`), open the folder in
> VS Code or any editor, and extend it for your own case with your AI assistant. This README is written for
> both of you. Taking these tools further is a free service of
> [Sharpes Process Engineering](https://jsharpes-portfolio.netlify.app); the site's Ask box can point you to the right starting file.

**The main finding:** a well-mixed bulk hides the hazard. Unreacted EO dissolved in the batch is limited to
about 1.2 wt % (pressure-limited) and gives only a 4–12 K adiabatic rise, with no relief demand. The danger
is a **local EO-rich pocket** at the dip-leg outlet. EO keeps feeding into it while the mixer is off, and it
can react adiabatically by hundreds of kelvin once mixing resumes. The tool sizes that pocket from feed
rate, response time, and dip-leg geometry.

## Scenario and failure modes

Loss of mixing (agitator motor failure) with instantaneous onset during EO feed:

- **Mode A:** the ESD closes the EO feed on agitator failure. Pocket mass = feed rate × valve closing time
  + EO standing in the line downstream of the valve.
- **Mode B:** the ESD is not credited and the feed continues until the mixer restarts. Restarting blends
  the pocket into the bulk and starts its reaction.
- **Mode C:** power outage. The feed stops because it loses motive power, regardless of the ESD. Cooling is lost.

## Method

1. **Batch state** from PEG grade, batch progress, temperature, and pressure. EO solubility in the
   EG-(EO)n polyether uses **UNIFAC** activity coefficients (γ_EO falls from 1.49 at n = 0 to 0.09 at
   n = 30, at 140 °C). Raoult's law badly overpredicts EO pressure for long chains.
2. **Pocket geometry:** dip-leg ID sized from feed rate at 0.255 m/s design velocity, back-derived from the
   Chiu thesis plant data (40.9 mm / 18 kg/min, 52.5 mm / 28 kg/min). Carrier volume = pipe cross-section ×
   stagnant-zone length. The pocket sits submerged near the vessel bottom, below the lowest impeller.
3. **Kinetics:** primary-OH rate constant k11 (Di Serio 1996, octanol proxy): ln A = 20.52,
   E = 13.0 kcal/mol, with an adjustable kinetic multiplier. Heat of reaction 92 kJ/mol EO.
   Decomposition is coupled at −132 kJ/mol (Crocco 1959 gas-phase kinetics), and a ~200 °C conservative
   decomposition-risk flag is shown.
4. **Pocket → bulk:** the reacting pocket exchanges heat with the bulk through a blend-time constant.
   A vessel energy and mass balance with vapor-liquid equilibrium gives P(t) and T(t). The relief rate W(t)
   follows once P reaches set pressure.
5. **Relief sizing:** Leung omega two-phase flux, API 520 orifice letter, with a rupture-disk option (K_c).
   It also reports alternatives and a normal-operation consistency check.

## Reference result

PEG 400, 6 m³, 50 % batch progress, 120 °C, 3.62 bar(a), MAWP 4.45 bar, feed 16 kg/min:

- ESD response 15–60 s: no relief demand
- Mode B: relief begins at about a 9.4 min restart delay
- 10 min delay: 2.9 kg/s (orifice H)
- 15 min delay: 18.9 kg/s (orifice N)
- Cooling-lost case at 15 min: 27.0 kg/s, bulk to 226 °C, above the ~200 °C decomposition markers

## Verification

- EO mass balance closes to 10⁻¹³.
- The no-vent energy balance is consistent.
- γ interpolation was checked against `thermo`.
- Perry's property functions were spot-checked against literature (EO density at 100 °C within 1 %;
  EG density within 0.3 %; EG cp within 0.6 %).
- A headless-browser sweep of 180–252 input combinations produced no errors or NaN.
- Validation against the Gakhar & Carr incident and a DIERS worked example is **not done yet**.

## Limits

- The k11 kinetics are an octanol proxy. The pocket regime (often more than 90 wt % EO) is a significant
  extrapolation. Use the kinetic multiplier to test sensitivity.
- The decomposition onset is unresolved: Crocco extrapolation (dormant below ~350 °C) vs. APTAC and BASF
  data (~200 °C).
- Results above the EO critical temperature (469 K) are diagnostic only.
- Stagnant-zone length and line inventory have no source and are flagged as advanced inputs.
- Homogeneous vent flow is assumed. The Baker Petrolite incident suggests real flow was not homogeneous.
